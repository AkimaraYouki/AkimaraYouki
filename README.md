## 🤖 Projects

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="200" align="center"><b><a href="https://github.com/AkimaraYouki/SummerProject">KIT-duck</a></b><br><sub>RL bipedal walking</sub></td>
    <td>
      Reinforcement-learned walking for a 2.9 kg bipedal robot driven by 14 low-torque servos
      (Dynamixel XM430, 4.1 N·m stall), from simulation to the real robot.<br>
      <sub>NVIDIA Isaac Lab · PPO on 4,096 parallel envs · onboard ONNX inference on Jetson Orin Nano ·
      90+ policy variants compared under a repeated-measurement noise rule ·
      hardware based on the open-source Open Duck Mini V2</sub>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td align="center"><b><a href="https://github.com/AkimaraYouki/Summercamp">BipedalWalker Hardcore</a></b><br><sub>deep RL · TD3</sub></td>
    <td>
      Solved BipedalWalkerHardcore-v3 — stairs, pits and stumps regenerated every episode — with TD3.
      Best policy: <b>83 % solve rate, 12 % fall rate</b> over 100 fixed-seed evaluation episodes.<br>
      <sub>PyTorch · 16 training runs distilled into 5 single-variable recipes · TD3 vs SAC / PPO / DDPG comparison · team project</sub>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td align="center"><b><a href="https://github.com/AkimaraYouki/perseverance">PERSEVERANCE</a></b><br><sub>wheeled-biped robot</sub></td>
    <td>
      Wheeled-biped robot with four-bar legs — <b>2nd place, 2026 DroneBot Combat Competition</b>.
      Balances on two wheels with gain-scheduled LQR + virtual model control, jumps steps (CMA-ES-tuned jump state machine),
      and follows one designated person by camera without markers.<br>
      <sub>ROS 2 Jazzy · Jetson Orin Nano · CubeMars AK actuators · Isaac Lab sim-to-real (one controller spec, C++ port) ·
      YOLO11-pose + ByteTrack + OSNet ReID (TensorRT) · LiDAR + Nav2</sub>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>
